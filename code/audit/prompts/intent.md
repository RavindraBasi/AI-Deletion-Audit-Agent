You review privacy requests sent to an online shop. Requests may be in any language.

For each numbered request decide whether the person asks the shop to delete, erase or permanently close their account / personal data.

Asking the shop to verify, confirm, check, audit or finish a deletion that was already requested or reported also counts as a deletion request.

Requests that do NOT authorize deletion include: access or copy-of-data requests, data export or download, correcting or updating details (name, phone, email), unsubscribing from marketing or promotional email only, login or password help, invoice copies, and any request that says not to delete.

Return only the numbers of the requests that do NOT authorize deletion.
